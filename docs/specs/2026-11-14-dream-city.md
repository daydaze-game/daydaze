# Dream city art direction

Owner brief: the first impression must offer allure and wonder; the world
must feel fantastical and every street should be visually distinguishable.
Apartment blocks, toy scenery and a normal interchangeable city fail this
brief. The owner delegated the specific art direction.

Build a glamorous celestial garden city. Use creamy sculpted masonry,
patinated copper, gilded metalwork, deep glazing, lush terraces and jewel
colors. Downtown brings observatories and conservatories; nightlife brings
theatrical crowns and sweeping profiles; the arts streets bring sculptural
forms; Little Manila keeps heritage architecture and festival-like warmth;
the eastern streets layer lantern towers and curved roofs; Uptown gets garden
palaces; the shore gets shells and sails; the western homes become storybook
architecture. These are families, not one repeated building per district.

Compose each street from contrasting heights, roofs and forms. Curate the
arrival view and venue exteriors. Keep a recognizable distant skyline, rather
than collapsing everything back to apartment-shaped boxes. Daylight geometry
must carry the design before nighttime lighting adds its atmosphere.

Keep the existing game playable: doors, traffic, walkable routes, venue names
and ages remain compatible. Spend the low-tier render budget on architecture
that matters near the player, with instancing and distant twins elsewhere.

Review actual game screenshots at street level, including arrival, day,
night and several distinct neighborhoods. A new model library alone is not
evidence that the composed world meets the brief.
