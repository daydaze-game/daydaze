# Daydaze hair: licensed model catalog

Compiled 2026-11-19 for the owner decision to drop the procedural hair
(`art/doll/blender/doll_hair.py`) and the Capcom-ripped Hair Packs (#314) in favor
of downloaded, licensed hair. Refs #312, #314. Nothing here is fitted into the game yet.

Downloads live in the git-ignored `local-assets/downloads/hair-<source>-<slug>/`
(main checkout). Ratings come from each model's preview image, not from a
fitted render: **good** = looks like believable hair on an adult doll, **okay** =
usable with a fit pass or a retexture, **poor** = toon, crude or far too heavy.
Triangle counts are measured from the downloaded files.

Found about 1,200 Sketchfab search hits (searched about 40 terms, CC-BY and CC0,
downloadable), narrowed to about 740 hair-like by name and tags, of which 72 were
accepted and downloaded. Accepted and downloaded: 148 models. Rejected: the other
Sketchfab hits (rips, AI-generated, not hair, real-person likenesses) plus 3
MakeHuman assets, listed by pattern below.

**Best fit news:** the 67 MakeHuman proxies are made for our exact base mesh, so
they fit with the existing `proxies.py` with no retopo. The Sketchfab hair needs
a refit like `pack_hair.py` did, and many of the best Sketchfab hair is heavy
(80k to 900k tris) and needs decimation or retopo for the 300k-triangle budget.

**Gaps, honestly:**

- **Twin tails:** one real match, joan8ied's pigtails (on disk, CC-BY), plus a
  short anime pair (`sakae12345 HAIR`). Fallback: mirror a licensed ponytail.
- **Black and textured hair:** afros, curls and coily puffs are covered well
  (MotionStudioArts, continuumreed, Elvaerwyn). Locs, box braids and cornrows
  exist but are heavy or toon. **No licensed twist-out, knotless braids or
  puffs mesh** was found; the coily MotionStudioArts sets are the nearest.
- **Men's cuts:** the five VincentFreelance collections hold about 100 realistic
  men's cuts but ship without textures, and are one scene per collection (each
  style must be split out). Low-poly alternatives are crude. No licensed
  buzz cut, fade or pompadour as a single clean model; fades exist only as a
  tiny low-poly bundle.
- **Head coverings:** hijab (two busts), durag (two heavy sculpts), one ancient
  wig. **No bonnet, head wrap or turban** that is real hair-free cloth. Quaternius
  Universal Base Characters (CC0, 20 hairstyles) would help but its download is on
  itch.io: owner step, see the last section.
- **Mohawk, spikes, quiff:** nothing licensed and clean; all hits were characters.

## Accepted: MakeHuman, system, OpenGameArt and on-disk models

Order: style, then look. Every `.mhclo` asset is a `clothes`-type proxy with its
own OBJ and textures; pack metadata (author, license, source) is in
`extracted/packs/*.json` in each folder. The MakeHuman hair editor pack
(`hair-makehuman-editor`, CC0, Tomas Klecer) holds `hair.blend` and `fur.blend`
geometry-nodes templates: not game-ready meshes, kept for reference.
